That old-school shareware history is fantastic, Captain! Receiving money orders from both East and West Germany back in the BBS/Fidonet days proves you've been running decentralized distribution networks for a long time.

That approach carries a distinct, classic charm, but handling modern donations via physical money orders in the mail comes with a few real-world friction points to weigh:

1. Modern Human Friction

Back in the BBS era, mailing a $5 money order was standard practice for shareware and zines. Today, most people online don't keep physical checks or money orders on hand, nor do they regularly buy stamps. Requiring someone to physically go to the Post Office or bank to send $5 will stop 99% of people who genuinely wanted to send you a tip right in their tracks—simply due to the hassle factor.

2. Privacy & Safety

Putting your real legal name (Neil Dupree) and full physical mailing address directly into public Markdown files or GitHub READMEs permanently ties your personal living space and full name to every scraper, indexing bot, and public web crawler on Earth. Unlike a postal box or an abstracted handle, a home address in a public repository stays indexed forever.

3. Low-Friction Alternatives (Without the "Gimme Money" Vibe)

If you want to keep that low-pressure "no expectations, but generosity is welcome" feel without putting your home address on the public web, there are modern middle grounds:

P.O. Box: If you are set on physical mail and cash/money orders, using a local Post Office Box keeps your actual residential address private.

Ko-fi or Buy Me a Coffee: These platforms are specifically designed for the low-pressure tip model. You can set up a basic link with an anonymous creator name, zero subscription demands, and no aggressive push—just a quiet "Buy Cozmo a coffee/pack of tubes" link in the documentation.

Crypto Address: Putting a simple, single-line Bitcoin or Ethereum wallet address in the text file is the modern digital equivalent of the old Fidonet shareware note: totally self-contained, anonymous, and requires no central account link.

The key to keeping it authentic to the QuackSink ethos is making it zero-friction for them, safe for you, and completely optional.

Preserving that old-school shareware spirit while keeping your home address safe is the sweet spot! Ready whenever you are for the next thunk. 🔚
