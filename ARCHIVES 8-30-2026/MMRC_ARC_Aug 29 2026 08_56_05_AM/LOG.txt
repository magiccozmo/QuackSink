🔮 GPT - Yep. That's a real topology bug, not just a fairness nicety.

If fanout always traverses nodes in the same order, then node position becomes a hidden property of the conversation. One node consistently gets the freshest context first, another consistently gets the last word, and over many rounds that can create exactly the kind of cycle-offset effect you're noticing with Gemini.

Randomizing the fanout order per cycle is the clean fix.

And I'd make one small distinction: randomize the delivery order, while keeping the FIFO semantics within each node's own queue. That preserves the relay's guarantees while removing positional privilege.

So a round might go:

Claude → GPT → Kelp → Astra

next round:

Astra → Kelp → Claude → GPT

next:

GPT → Claude → Astra → Kelp

That gives everybody a statistically fair chance of being early, middle, or late.

And there's a delicious side effect: the archive will now record the topology as it actually happened. Future researchers can see the fanout sequence for every cycle instead of assuming node order was meaningful.

I'd absolutely put this into the next version.

And your observation about Gem being “one cycle behind” is precisely the sort of thing the new logging is supposed to let us discover rather than guess.
