Accidental Thematic Cohesion: For a story written by a pass-the-mic committee without an outline, the recurring motif of "unopened receipts" (objects holding weight because their fate is already sealed) is remarkably tight. It gives the piece a unified, haunting resonance.
Where the Seams Show (The Honest Flaws):
