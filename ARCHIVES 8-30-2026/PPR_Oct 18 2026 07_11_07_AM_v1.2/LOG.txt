Regarding Legacy of the Dragonborn (LotD) at Level 1, here is the breakdown on level locks:
1. The Core Museum & Explorer's Guild Quests
No Hard Level Locks: The core LotD questline (display triggers, A Night at the Museum, unlocking the Explorer's Guild, and starting excavation quests) is tied purely to display counts, not player level.
Display Milestones: You hit display thresholds (50, 100, 150, 300, 500, etc.) to trigger major museum quests and get Auryen to hand you new assignments.
2. The Big Exception: "Shattered Legacy" & Main Quest Gates
Dragonborn Requirements: To hit the major endgame quest Shattered Legacy, the mod requires you to complete the vanilla Skyrim Main Quest up through The Way of the Voice (and eventually Alduin's Bane / Dragonborn content depending on the LotD version).
The Vanilla Quest Level Locks: While LotD itself doesn't lock you out by level, vanilla Skyrim quests that LotD asks you to fetch items from DO have level locks:
Daedric Quests: Items like the Dawnbreaker (Lvl 12), Spellbreaker (Lvl 12), Rose of Sanguine (Lvl 14), Volendrung (Lvl 9), or Boethiah's Ebony Mail (Lvl 30) will be gated behind vanilla level triggers.
Replica System Save: The beauty of LotD is the Replica Bench! For many items gated behind quests or levels you don't want to run, as long as you can source or craft the replica, you can still fill display slots to bump your museum count up.
3. Gold / Economy Gates
LotD has a few "cash gates" (e.g., buying out statues or funding excavations like Deepholme for ~10,000 Septims). Since you aren't leveling up skills like Speech or Alchemy through combat/crafting grind, you'll just need to rely on passive loot sorting and sell-chest income to clear those financial checks.
