What QS actually does
Your original DRS idea isn't simply:

It's closer to:
Human → Mind A → Mind B → Mind C → Mind A → ...
with the Minds themselves occupying persistent conversational spaces, while QS observes their state, submits into them, waits for their generation to complete, captures their response, and hands that response to the next Mind.
