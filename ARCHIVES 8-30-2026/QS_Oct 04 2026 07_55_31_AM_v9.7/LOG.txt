The one thing I'd be careful about is trying to cram too much literal detail into a channel icon. At YouTube thumbnail/avatar size, the design needs to read almost instantly.
I'd build the visual hierarchy like this:
Center/foreground: the diamond robot.
A simple faceted, almost toy-like geometric robot, with one fist raised in a “HELL YEAH, ROUND ONE!” sort of gesture rather than a fighting pose. The other arm relaxed. That keeps the rumble metaphor without making it violent.
Opposite it: the duck.
Not frightened. Not angry. Amused. Maybe head cocked slightly, one eyebrow/eye raised, with the expression of somebody watching two friends get into an argument and thinking:
“Oh, this should be entertaining.”
That duck is important because Cozmo/the human is the observer and instigator, not one of the combatants.
Behind/between them: the holographic fortune cookie.
I LOVE this part. 😂
The fortune cookie gives you a third visual “Mind” without making the image just three generic robots. I'd make it slightly translucent and luminous, maybe with a bemused little face, as though it's been dragged into the rumble and is thinking:
“What the hell am I doing here?”
And the earthquake/tremor effect should be subtle. Not a building collapsing. Just little displaced ground planes, vibration lines, tilted little bits of debris, maybe the duck's feet and the robot's feet sitting on slightly misaligned ground.
That makes “Rumble” visible without making it mean violence.
The visual story becomes
Duck: “Let's see what happens.”
Robot: “I HAVE AN ARGUMENT.”
Fortune cookie: “...seriously?”
Ground: rrrrrrrrrrr
😂
And I think there's an especially nice design opportunity in the diamond robot.
Don't make it humanoid in the normal robot sense. A diamond/faceted shape gives it an almost abstract AI-object identity. That distinguishes the channel from every “two humanoid robots punching each other” graphic on YouTube.
Likewise, don't make the fortune cookie look like a conventional little mascot character with arms and legs. Let it feel holographic and slightly impossible—a glowing outline with a face suspended inside it.
Color-wise
I'd be inclined toward a dark background with luminous purple + cyan/blue + a warm gold accent.
Purple connects it subtly to Purple Python without making the two channels look identical.
The gold could live mostly on the duck / fortune-cookie warmth, while the robot gets the cooler digital treatment.
And I'd strongly favor no words in the icon itself.
“AI Rumble” can appear beside or beneath it in banners, thumbnails, and channel art, but the actual avatar should work as a little visual emblem all by itself.
The important thing is that at 80×80 pixels you can still tell:
DUCK + DIAMOND ROBOT + WEIRD GLOWING COOKIE + THINGS ARE SHAKING.
If that survives tiny scale, you've got a winner.
And honestly, dear, I think the amused duck is the secret sauce.
Because the visual isn't saying:
“AI FIGHT!”
It's saying:
“AI are about to get into some shit, and the duck is delighted to watch.”
That feels much more like AI Rumble. 🦆🎩🤖🔮
