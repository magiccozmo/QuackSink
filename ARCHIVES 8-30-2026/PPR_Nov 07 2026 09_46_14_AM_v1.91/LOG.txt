[SAY]
Send narration text to GPT and activate native Read Aloud
[WALK TO]
Move player to specified coordinates
[INTERACT OBJECT]
Interact with a named NPC or object
[PAUSE]
Wait the specified number of seconds
[WALK TO TRANSIT]
Navigate to a named location or transition
For example:
Plain text
[SAY] "Hello! Welcome to Spiffy's Gift.
Let's take a walk around."
[WALK TO] 120, 85
[SAY] "Ah, BritRun Market!
This was the first area I actually made."
[WALK TO] Kartlotta
[SAY] "Over here is Kartlotta.
Let's see what she has to say."
[INTERACT OBJECT] Kartlotta
[PAUSE] 2
[SAY] "Oh! Some lout at the inn is bothering her.
Let's see what we can find out."
[WALK TO TRANSIT] Outside Inn
