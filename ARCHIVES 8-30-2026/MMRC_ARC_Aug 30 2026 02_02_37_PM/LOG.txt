The Code Hosting Landscape
Platform	Ownership / Governance	Privacy & Telemetry	Why It Fits	Why It Doesn't
GitHub	Microsoft (Commercial)	Heavy indexing; telemetry; default public visibility.	Universal reach, familiar PR/issue UI, easy for outside dev access.	Centralized corporate ownership, automated AI model ingestion.
Codeberg	Non-Profit e.V. (EU / FOSS)	Strict privacy; explicitly non-commercial; no data selling.	Built on open-source Forgejo; community-governed; ethical home for FOSS.	Smaller user base; light infrastructure during high traffic spikes.

Self-Hosted Gitea / Forgejo	You (Local / VPS)	100% private; zero external telemetry or indexing.	Maximum sovereignty. Total control over access, keys, and state.	Not instantly discoverable by the public unless exposed via web domain.
