We must design the system of interacting state machines first, then let Python implement that system.
For SGGA, that means:
World state: Areas, objects, player position, inventory, and ownership are defined as data and relationships.
Asset state: A JSON asset definition identifies artwork and its relevant properties. The game loads those definitions rather than relying on hard-coded drawings for its world.
Interaction state: Hovering, selecting, picking up, stealing, and dropping objects follow explicit rules.
Transition state: Doors connect areas through defined relationships. Future paths, roads, and screen-edge transitions can use the same general architecture.
