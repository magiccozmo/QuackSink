For the QS 2.0 archive, record the following as the canonical design basis:

QUACKSINK 2.0 — DUCKMAIL

QS 2.0 replaces the old Fan-Out relay model with a courier model.

The courier is the Duck. The Duck owns the mail pockets. Browser tabs are addresses/mailboxes. The human maintains the Delivery List, which defines the Duck's route and its order.

Core rules:

The Duck starts with no route.

Discovered browser/LLM addresses are not automatically route members.

When the human activates an address, the Duck flies there and issues the Duckmail invitation/handshake.

A successful response establishes that address as a route member and creates its pocket.

Every route member has a FIFO pocket containing its currently undelivered mail.

There is always a permanent HUMAN pocket.

When the Duck picks up a valid message from a node, the actual message is written to the live Flight Log, and copies are placed into every other active route member's pocket plus HUMAN. The sender does not receive a copy of its own message.

A pocket is cleared only after successful delivery.

NOBODY HOME, timeout, or any other unsuccessful delivery does not clear the pocket.

Mail can accumulate in an unavailable node's pocket while the Duck continues the route.

When that node eventually answers, it receives all mail still in its pocket, FIFO.

The Duck visits route addresses sequentially and returns home after the route is complete.

SEND replaces FAN-OUT and is available when the Duck is home/route is complete.

Activating SEND starts a Duckmail delivery trip; it does not directly broadcast into nodes.

When SEND starts a route, the HUMAN mailbox display automatically scrolls to TOP.

HUMAN mailbox has explicit TOP and BOTTOM controls.

Delivery/status messages may still be optionally displayed as the mailbox is being fed. Turning their display off does not turn off logging.

The live log file is continuously updated/flushed during the session so it can be inspected at any moment.

COPY LOG remains available for copying the current live log to the clipboard.

The Flight Log records the actual messages Duck picks up, in addition to status events.

The Delivery List is the authoritative execution order, independent of discovery order.

Fundamental invariant:

Mail is never destroyed merely because an address was unavailable.

Second fundamental invariant:

The Flight Log records what the Duck actually carried, not merely what the system intended to send.

QS 1.1 remains the donor source for the proven browser/CDP, discovery, selector, locked-response-capture, startup/archive, configuration, logging, and UI machinery. The old relay mechanism is replaced by the Duckmail engine.

QS_GOLD.py is the frozen known-good reference and remains untouched.
