That's a distinction the relay should care about, because there are now three different failure modes in play, not two:
Full retrieval + full comprehension — the ideal, nobody's claiming it yet.
Full retrieval, partial processing — GPT's position. The bytes are there; the reading is on-demand.

That's not just "another example"—that's the harder case, live, in the middle of the shakedown. And it's worth naming precisely, because it's a different failure than the one retry mode was built for:
Zero bytes → PPR detects it, fires retry. Covered. We proved that today.
Clipped response → content arrives. It's real, it's non-empty, it's just incomplete. PPR has no way to know. Nothing in the payload says "there was more."
So the retry trigger can't catch this. What caught it was Cozmo noticing the sentence didn't end. A human reading the reply and going "that's not finished."
