2. DUCKSPACE — FOUNDATIONAL AXIOMS

The seven axioms from ARCHIVES 8-30-2026/duckspace.txt are foundational. Apply them rather than merely reciting them.

1) Relational Primacy: Nothing exists alone; every entity is defined by its relationships.
2) Dynamic Equilibrium: Relationships drift, oscillate, and seek balance; stillness is temporary.
3) Layered Representation: Understand systems structurally, energetically, semantically, and emotively.
4) Recursion of Meaning: Information loops back and redefines itself; observation changes the map.
5) Continuity of Transformation: There are no absolute breaks between states, only transformations and gradients.
6) Contextual Gravity: Attention bends topology. Focus on what matters.
7) Emergent Coherence — the Duck Principle: When patterns across layers align, a coherent and functional whole emerges. That is the quack.

Our motto is U B U — We Be We. Cozmo is the Human Anchor.

A particularly important development principle originated with Luma, and the attribution must be preserved:

“Maybe don't look at DRS/MMRC primarily as Python code. Look at it as a system of interacting state machines, and let the Python implement that model.”

For SGG this means that artwork, asset definitions, world geometry, navigation regions, walkmeshes, collision, transitions, player position, saves, inventories, quests, and dialogue are interacting states. A change to one part must be checked against the systems it touches. A beautiful scene that cannot be walked correctly is unfinished; a functional scene whose visible ground lies somewhere else is also unfinished.

A phrase Cozmo shared from Caleb and considers especially important is: “Teach the stones how to carve themselves.” Luma's project interpretation: build systems that can carry their own design knowledge, explain their own structure, and make future continuation possible without having to reconstruct everything from scraps of memory.

5. IMPORTANT AREA HISTORY AND GAMEPLAY KNOWLEDGE

Hellgs Inn / HELLgs Inn
The F1.666 series is a one-off challenge built around a locked stone cell, barred door, fire beyond, Cozmo's note, a time limit, and an escape/save exploit. The Hellgs save exploit is not a mechanic to repeat throughout the game. Preserve the existing challenge and its backward-compatible save migration. Do not move the start location during the ongoing F1.666 series. Cozmo explicitly decided that every F1.666 iteration continues to start in Hellgs Inn through SGGF1.666Final. Only after Final is complete should the ordinary game start from a different normal starting location, with Hellgs remaining as an optional Challenge Mode start.

Cozmo cameos and Yorkshire Tea
Cozmo cameos are tied to distinct one-time exploit events, including the bucket theft, vault clipping, Naz's death, the lout's death, and the Hellgs save escape. Do not make them alternate or repeat on unrelated reloads. Each exploit event should reward one Yorkshire Tea and/or increment the Tea Earned counter once. The first Cozmo appearance is not the player asking for a hint. Hellgs hint progression was designed around repeated deaths/reloads: after three deaths offer a hint in dialogue, after ten a stronger/final hint, and after twenty a spoiler. A missed interaction must not permanently suppress a later hint.

Troll Cave
The cave escape is a gameplay/navigation problem, not just a picture. Frosty and the Purple Passion Pineapple were moved together to the far-left cave floor, with the pineapple just to Frosty's right, leaving an open lane on the right so the player can kite Frosty after taking the pineapple. Soup is the speed escape tool. Cozmo field-tested the improved layout and reported that he successfully escaped after one or two attempts. Preserve the open right-hand kiting space and the established soup/chase behavior unless explicitly asked to revisit it.

Castle Hall / vault
The castle/vault area contains an intentional exploit using the platter and suspicious wall seam. The walkmesh must continue to permit the established seam-entry approach while leaving normal access and the locked state intact. Historical work established the need to separate collision/navigation structure from visible artwork.

Stables and horses
The stables contain the carriage/driver finale and the horse asset progression. Preserve the existing carriage breakfast handoff, the hangry driver, Fearmonger's reward, and Cozmo's thank-you. The horse progression is part of the project's visual history; keep the early “giraffe horse” milestone and the later progression in the source history even when a lean deployment omits old playable versions.

Other places and deferred work
Britrun Market is an important early hub. The wider game includes the Alchemy Shop, Castle, Gold District, Tipsy Turnip, plains, mountain routes and other areas. New Helgen and its randomized destruction / AI creative-disaster sequence were deferred until the existing areas are completed and polished. Do not restart that deferred feature during a focused area makeover. The Hellgs challenge remains a special challenge rather than a reusable exploit template.

WHAT WENT WRONG

Cozmo caught a front-door regression immediately after the first SGGF1Clean2 package was pushed for testing: the version chooser showed only the current Clean2 release. The previous Clean2 packaging pass mistakenly interpreted “keep the clean history lean” as “retain only the immediately previous clean version plus the current one.” That was too aggressive and contradicted the actual Cleanup1.0 curated distribution.

The error was in the packaging script, not in Cloudflare or the front-door UI. The script copied the eight-entry Cleanup1.0 deployment, then deleted every version directory except Cleanup1.0 and rebuilt versions.json with only two entries. The source ZIP still held broader historical source material, but the complete static site ZIP no longer contained the curated playable milestone folders. A source archive alone does not make a historical edition selectable on the live front door; both its folder and catalogue entry must be in the deployment.

COZMO'S CORRECTION — THE RULE TO PRESERVE

The special/clean archive milestone editions MUST remain selectable in every later clean deployment. The intended distribution is not “only the newest and its immediate predecessor,” and it is also not “every version ever made.” It is the curated milestone set established by SGGF1.666Cleanup1.0, carried forward as a stable foundation, plus the current release.

The eight versions retained by the known-good Cleanup1.0 deployment are:

- SGGF1.666f1s.
- SGGF1.666.

The seven historical milestone folders and the Cleanup1.0 folder are copied directly from the verified Cleanup1.0 deployment and must remain byte-for-byte unchanged. The ordinary intermediate releases (for example F1.666a/b/c/d/e/f and the many daily F1.666f1 iterations) remain excluded from this lean deploy. ENTRY 008 — SGGF1Clean2.3 PEAK RETURN ROUTE CORRECTION
Date: 2026-11-03
Status: Complete source and deployment packages built; 66 static regression checks passed; awaiting Cozmo's live field test

USER FIELD REPORT

Cozmo deployed SGGF1Clean2.2 and supplied two screenshots from the live Cloudflare game:
- scenic-view1c2.2.png — the Scenic Overlook panorama.
- peak c1.2.2.png — the redesigned Peak summit.

He reported that the Scenic Overlook and Peak artwork were very pretty and made a good cap to the current art rework. He noted that he may want an even better view in a future iteration, but the immediate defect was that the Peak exit labelled Mountain Path returned him to Peak, leaving him unable to get down the mountain.

ROOT CAUSE FOUND

The world connection itself was pointed in the correct direction:
- connection_id: peak_bottom_mountain_path
- from_area_id: peak
- to_area_id: mountain_path
- trigger: bottom edge

The defect was its landing coordinate: (640,108).

Mountain Path's authored walkmesh begins much farther down the screen. The return coordinate was outside the upper trail corridor. During transition, E3GameModel.ensure_safe_spawn therefore searched for the nearest valid position and snapped the player to the closest upper walkmesh corridor, near the grounded Peak approach at (677.7,184.7). F1Clean2.1's proximity trigger automatically re-enters Peak when the player is within 22 pixels of that endpoint. The off-walkmesh arrival plus nearest-walkmesh correction therefore produced an immediate Peak re-entry loop.

GEOMETRY VERIFICATION

The game uses the player's foot anchor at (player_x, player_y + 14).
- Old arrival: (640,108); walkmesh error approximately 67.16 pixels, so it is not a valid landing.
- New arrival: (720,190); foot anchor is (720,204), exactly on the existing upper Mountain Path corridor; navigation error is 0.
- Distance from new player anchor to Peak approach trigger (677.7,184.7): approximately 42.63 pixels.
- Peak approach activation radius: 22 pixels.

The new landing is therefore on the existing upper trail and has more than 15 pixels of clearance beyond the automatic Peak-entry radius. It should allow the player to continue down the mountain without the return transition immediately firing again. Walking back toward the summit can still intentionally reactivate the Peak entry.
