- The two title lines are now scaled against the board's inset face, with both width and row height constrained, so large UI font settings cannot overflow the sign.
- K1.9's lowered Mage Lab arch art, K1.8 Castle Hall/Exterior release guard and landing points, and K1.7 Player Says/City Gate input safeguards are retained.
