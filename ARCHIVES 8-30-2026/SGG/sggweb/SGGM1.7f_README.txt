SGGM1.7f — TROLL CAVE GETS ITS K1.12 ICY CAVERN BACK

Built from the exact SGGM1.7e source and complete Cloudflare-site archives, using the K1.12 cave presentation as the visual reference.

This release replaces the M1 Troll Cave's brown procedural placeholder room with the original c1_bg_troll_cave Luma Draw Engine artwork at its authored 1280x640 dimensions. Frosty is restored as the original troll sprite at the K1.12 home position. The Purple Passion Pineapple is restored as its original Luma sprite beside Frosty. The old cave-floor navigation shape is approximated by connected walkmesh rectangles, and the exit trigger is centred on the painted cave opening.

The M1 countryside-to-cave connection remains in place; its arrival is moved to an open floor position matching the old K1.12 room layout. The cave's minimap, nearest-portal hint, NPC label and generic exit marker are hidden so the authored cavern remains visually faithful.

IMPORTANT SCOPE NOTE: this is a visual set-dressing and navigation-layout pass. The pineapple and Frosty are rendered from their original Luma assets, but no new object interaction, pickup, combat or NPC behavior is introduced. Existing M1 world logic outside this layer is unchanged.

The Tipsy Turnip restoration, Market Square, Alchemy Shop doorway correction, other areas, routes, fast travel, saves, logging and historical editions are preserved. No Worker or D1 logging infrastructure changed.
