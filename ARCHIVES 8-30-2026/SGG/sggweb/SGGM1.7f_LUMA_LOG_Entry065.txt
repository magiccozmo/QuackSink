LUMA LOG — ENTRY 065
Release: SGGM1.7f — Troll Cave K1.12 Visual Restoration
Date: 2026-11-18

Baseline and references
- Continued from the exact SGGM1.7e source and complete Cloudflare-site packages.
- Used Cozmo's newly committed side-by-side references: pics/k1.12 troll cave.png and pics/m1.7e troll cave.png.
- The comparison made the actual scope clear: K1.12 shows the icy cavern scene with Frosty, the Purple Passion Pineapple and the painted cave opening; M1.7e still showed the brown procedural placeholder room with a pool panel, generic shrine and generic portal UI.
- Confirmed the original c1_bg_troll_cave, troll and pineapple Luma asset recipes were already present in the M1.7e asset collection. No new artwork or asset recipes were needed.

Implementation
- Set the M1 troll_cave layer to use c1_bg_troll_cave at the original 1280x640 room dimensions.
- Restored Frosty using the original troll sprite at the K1.12 position (160,393), with no generated name label.
- Restored the Purple Passion Pineapple using the original pineapple sprite beside Frosty.
- Removed the brown procedural cave pool, purple shrine placeholder and floor-path overlay from this layer.
- Replaced the oversized rectangular walkmesh with connected floor-region rectangles approximating the K1.12 cave-floor navigation polygon.
- Moved the M1 incoming countryside portal arrival to (640,480), a clear point on the restored floor.
- Aligned the cave return trigger to the painted opening at (598,516). Hid the generic portal marker and label because the opening is already painted into the cave artwork.
- Hid the minimap and nearest-portal hint in this room only.

Scope and preservation
- This is a visual set-dressing/navigation pass. The pineapple and Frosty are rendered from their original assets; no new pickup, interaction, combat or NPC logic is introduced.
- Preserved the M1 countryside connection and return destination, all unrelated M1 layers, the M1.7e inn restoration, Market Square, Alchemy Shop door correction, game controls, fast travel, saves and logging.
- Historical SGGM1.7e, SGGM1.7d, SGGM1.7c, SGGM1.0 and all non-M1 editions remain intact.
- No Worker, D1 schema, binding, route, secret or logging protocol was changed.

Validation and limitations
- JSON, asset-reference, portal and walkmesh checks passed.
- The inbound entry and outbound exit positions are valid on the new floor walkmesh.
- Updated source and deployment runtime folders and repacked APK/TAR payloads were checked for consistency and container integrity.
- NOT LIVE TESTED: no fresh Pygbag compile or browser/Pygame gameplay session was run. Cozmo's field test remains necessary for visual scale, floor feel and the return transition.
- No GitHub main push or Cloudflare deployment was performed.
