K1.6 FIELD-TEST FINDINGS ADDRESSED

1. Player Says keyboard isolation
- K1.6 intercepted composer keyboard events and passed zero elapsed time to the base update method, but an inherited E40/E41 update layer still ran and read physical keyboard state. This allowed held movement input to reach world/door logic while the text composer was visible.
- K1.7 now returns immediately from the state-machine update while Player Says is open. No inherited update layer, movement handler, NPC tick, or door detector runs during composition.
- K1.7 tracks keys pressed while the composer is open and creates a post-composer release barrier. All keyboard events are swallowed until keys held through composition have been released. The barrier is also checked against the live Pygame pressed-key snapshot to clear safely if focus changes.
- Player Says remains private and local. Submit still records to the local session log and exportable flight recorder only; no Global Usage Log event call or shared worker change was added.
- Text input is explicitly stopped when the composer closes and restarted when it opens.

2. Front Gate / Outside Gate ping-pong
- K1.6 arrivals placed the player at Front Gate (640,490) and Outside Gate (640,450). These positions allowed the same held northward movement to activate the reciprocal door immediately after the 0.32-second door cooldown.
- Both city-gate connection arrivals are now (640,520). The selected point is inside the walkable central ground region in both areas and is clear of the Front Gate solid rectangles.
- After either city-gate transition, the paired city-gate door detector remains disarmed while any configured directional movement key (or arrow key) is held. It re-arms only after all directional movement keys are released. The existing short cooldown is retained as an additional guard, not used as the sole protection.
- The change is limited to the two City Gate connection arrival positions and City Gate re-arm behavior. Other doors, routes, NPC transit, world areas, and walkmesh definitions are untouched.
