YORKSHIRE TEA RULES

- Tea remains a counter only; no physical Tea inventory item is created.
- Tea Earned, detected reward-event IDs, and claimed reward-event IDs are stored in the current game's c1_flags. c1_flags is included in the existing forward-compatible save snapshot.
- Saving captures the Tea total and reward ledger; loading restores them from that save. Loading an earlier snapshot returns to its earlier total, rather than stacking another point above that snapshot. A genuinely new game starts Tea Earned at zero.
- Easy mode auto-awards exactly one Tea when a designated reward cameo appears.
- Normal and Spiffy Challenge require the player to talk to the specific rewarding Cozmo cameo. If the player leaves the cameo's area first, it disappears and that reward is forfeited for the loaded game state.
- Each of the six reward-bearing cameos has unique dialogue explaining its one-Tea reward: bucket theft, Naz's unexpected justice, vault clip, inn-lout resolution, HELLgs escape, and first caravan-chest loot.
- Scenic-overlook Cozmo remains a non-rewarding appreciation cameo.
- HELLgs escape rewards are restricted to Spiffy Challenge.

CAMEO TIMING

- The base D1.1 scheduler and the F1.666c exploit-cameo scheduler now queue ordinary Cozmo appearances one in-game second after detection, down from five.
- Existing dialogue, death, active-cameo, safe-area, and event-location safeguards remain.
