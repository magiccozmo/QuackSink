The new market is integrated into the M1.6a continuous-world coordinates. Existing paths, walkmesh and portals are unchanged. K1.12 market item identity and theft/permission metadata are carried forward; M1.7 currently renders these as static display props. The Market Guard is present visually, but no death-by-guard mechanics are activated.

No live Pygbag build or browser playtest was available during this packaging pass. The runtime TAR/ APK payloads are repackaged from the M1.6a payload with the new M1.7 main.py, world JSON and Luma assets; they were not freshly compiled by Pygbag.
