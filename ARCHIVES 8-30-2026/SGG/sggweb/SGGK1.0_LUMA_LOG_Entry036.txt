Date: 2026-11-08
Baseline: SGGJ1.0, exact source and complete-site archives verified against repository main commit 0c23d1a8ed1ea01029ec1da76f328166489f9f65.

PURPOSE

Create the first playable foundation for The Little Lantern orphanage: an entrance at the player-marked left-hand house doorway in Front Gate, a new interior, caretaker Grella, and three children. The work is deliberately limited to this foundation; later Grella quest events remain deferred.

THE LITTLE LANTERN

- Added a separate world area named little_lantern_interior, displayed as The Little Lantern.
- Added a visible brass-plaque entrance on the existing left-hand Front Gate house doorway and a walk-through trigger matched to the approach from the paved frontage.
- Added a reciprocal interior exit returning to Front Gate.
- Added original Luma Draw Engine background art: warm plaster and timber, windows, hearth and fire, wall art, shelves, bunks, children's belongings, toy corner, rug, shared table, and a central clear path.
- Added custom entrance and exit door art.

RESIDENTS

Grella the Hearthkeeper is a genuinely kind, practical caretaker. Her dialogue welcomes the player and describes the home. The three children each have distinctive art and dialogue:
- Miri: bookish, curious, and interested in stories and maps.
- Tuck: exuberant, imaginative, and practicing to become a knight.
- Wren: shy and observant, with a plush rabbit named Button.

IMPLEMENTATION

- Added four NPC definitions and four separate asset recipes to the JSON world.
- Added the K1.0 dialogue/state layer and interface rendering layer while retaining the full J1.0 parent chain.
- The new area and NPCs use the existing world-default merge on save load. Existing saved state is not reset or replaced; the save schema is unchanged.
- Updated the front-gate special door renderer so the wide city gate retains its double-leaf art while a smaller house entrance can draw its own door.
- Kept all previous release directories, historic versions, quest mechanics, difficulty behavior, save schema, Giant Camp logic, and Global Usage Worker source.
- Updated fast-travel cell placement to keep all 23 areas within the existing two-column selector.
- Updated the runtime's global session identity to SGGK1.0.

EXPLICITLY NOT IMPLEMENTED

Grella's later follower/escort sequence, area stops, Giant Camp flight, Mudcrab River retreat/death, Tea award, and Grella MK2 remain future work. The cookpot Hole-in-One award and giant-flight text/reward changes also remain future work.
