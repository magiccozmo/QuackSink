GLOBAL LOG COMPATIBILITY FINDING

The I1.3 field export recorded "Session creation failed: Invalid game_version" because the deployed global-log Worker validator only accepted SGGG-prefixed versions. The included Worker source updates the validator to accept the SGG[A-Z] version family, including SGGJ1.0. The Worker is a separate Cloudflare service and requires a separate owner deployment; Luma has not deployed it.
