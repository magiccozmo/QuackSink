COZMO'S REQUEST

Extend the Inn Lout's existing player-follow behavior so that, when the lout is still alive, unresolved, and following the player, he can continue from BritRun Market through the city gate, Outside Gate, Stables, Plains and into Giant Camp. Cozmo's intention is to let the existing giant interaction launch the lout into the air instead of requiring the player to be the collision victim.

K1.11 CHANGE

- Added SGGK1_11StateMachine as a narrow subclass of the complete K1.10 state machine.
- The additional permitted follow destinations are front_gate, outside_gate, stables, plains and giant_camp. The existing Inn Interior → Inn Outside → BritRun Market behavior remains inherited from K1.10.
- The lout is transferred only when the player crosses a real connection whose source area matches the lout's current area, and only while the lout is alive, follow_player is true, and lout_resolved is unset.
- The lout is positioned relative to the player's arrival point, with the existing world-boundary clamping, NPC FOLLOW logging and short follow quip.
- The active game launch now instantiates the K1.11 state machine and interface. The K1.10 interface is inherited unchanged, including the Little Lantern sign rendering.
- The existing Giant Camp patrol, collision-victim selection, NPC collision detection and launch/flight sequence were not changed. Grella's separate fleeing exception was not changed.
