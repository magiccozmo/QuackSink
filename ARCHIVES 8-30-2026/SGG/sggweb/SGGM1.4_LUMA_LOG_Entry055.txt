THE PLAN

Peasant Way leaves the Square westward to Intersection A. Castle Path continues north from A to Castle Exterior. Easy Street runs north from Gold District to A. Snob Row runs west from just north of Little Lantern into Gold District. Little Lantern is directly west of the City Gate.
