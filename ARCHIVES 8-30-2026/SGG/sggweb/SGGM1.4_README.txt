SGGM1.4 — BritRun Street Plan (Placeholder Layout Pass)

Baseline: exact SGGM1.3a source and complete Cloudflare site archives retrieved from GitHub main and verified before editing.

This release refines only BritRun's city exterior street/building/NPC/portal layout to match Cozmo's named-road plan:
- Market Road runs north from the southeast City Gate and ends at Market Square.
- Kart-lotta and her stand occupy the centre of Market Square.
- Tipsy Turnip sits north of the Square, with its south-side door and a short approach from the Square.
- Alchemy Shop sits east of the Square, with its existing door and a short approach.
- Peasant Way runs west from the Square to Intersection A.
- Castle Path continues north from A to Castle Exterior and Castle Gate.
- Easy Street runs north from Gold District to A.
- Snob Row runs west from just north of Little Lantern into Gold District.
- Little Lantern remains a small placeholder building directly west of the City Gate, with a connected doorway approach.

The existing simple placeholder renderer is retained. No new artwork, sprites, backgrounds, images, or Luma drawing recipes were created or modified. The Little Lantern interior, Grella, and the three children are unchanged.

The corrected M1.3a startup anchor (city_exterior, 3400,1490), Easy/Normal Stables starts, and Spiffy Challenge HELLgs start are preserved. All 11 world layers and 19 directed portals remain. Four existing city NPC IDs and five city portal IDs remain unchanged; only city-side positions and five reciprocal return targets are revised to match the new layout.

No game logic, dialogue, NPC behavior, quests, combat, saves, controls, UI, Worker, D1, or Luma asset JSON changes.
